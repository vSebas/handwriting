# Handwriting-to-LaTeX components

The handwriting recognition feature includes adapted feature extraction and decoder logic,
and the vocabulary, from [Hand-to-TeX](https://github.com/Projekt-Deep-Learning-2026/hand-to-tex),
revision `cf27b992950eba3b1d3600bf18be44868d217917`.

Copyright (c) 2026 Projekt: Deep Learning 2026

The [htt-mini model](https://huggingface.co/m4jkiuwr/htt-mini) is downloaded separately
at revision `58170cc16748a5652e5e58caf93019fb8b0603c4`. Its model card declares MIT.

[ONNX Runtime](https://github.com/microsoft/onnxruntime), version 1.26.0,
is included in the plugin's recognition worker.
